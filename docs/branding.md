# Branding

## Name

**Moggie** — British for a mixed-breed cat. A pedigree speaker running someone else's
software, and none the worse for it.

Pronounced *MOG-ee*. Always capitalised as a proper noun, never `Moggy` and never all
caps except in the wordmark.

## Slogans

- **Primary:** Giving old speakers nine lives.
- **Strapline:** Designed in California. Purrfected in Teesside.

The strapline is a joke about where the hardware came from and where the software was
written. Keep the spelling of *Purrfected* — the pun is the point.

## Voice

Dry, British, affectionate. We are fond of the hardware and unimpressed by the software
that abandoned it. No hype, no exclamation marks, no "revolutionary". If a thing is hard,
say it is hard.

Good: *"The root fs budget is 7.5 MB. That is not negotiable."*
Bad: *"Unlock the ultimate potential of your smart speaker!!"*

## Palette

| Role | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Charcoal | `#12161C` | Backgrounds, always |
| Electric blue | `#2E8BFF` | Primary accent, left-side rim light |
| Violet | `#7B4DFF` | Secondary accent, right-side rim light, emphasis |
| Off-white | `#F2F4F8` | Text on dark |

No green. No gradients in the mark itself.

## Art direction

Dark, neon, screen-print. One light source from each side — blue left, violet right —
a glossy floor reflection, and visible grain. The subject is always the speaker and,
usually, a cat that has made itself at home on it.

Avoid: bright backgrounds, lens flares, 3D renders with plastic shine, stock-photo
smiles.

## Logo

The current mark direction is a cat head whose whiskers are audio waveform bars —
solid silhouette, single colour, readable at 32 px. It is not final and not yet in
the repo.

## Usage

- The hero image is [`assets/moggie-hero.png`](../assets/moggie-hero.png).
- Don't stretch, recolour, or place on a light background.
- Don't crop the cat's ears or the speaker's feet.
